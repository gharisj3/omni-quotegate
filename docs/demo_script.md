# Omni QuoteGate 2-Minute Demo Script

1. Open the dashboard and explain the banner: AI can draft and recommend, but only humans approve risky quote actions.
2. Open the inquiry inbox and point out the seeded WhatsApp-style Bosch Drill inquiry.
3. Select the Bosch Drill inquiry detail page.
4. Click Analyze inquiry to classify the request and show product/customer context.
5. Highlight the matched Bosch Drill X2200 record and the stock warning: 30 available versus 50 requested.
6. Click Draft quote to generate the customer-facing reply draft and risk flags.
7. Show that the quote is routed to approval because of stock review and discount review.
8. Open the approvals page and inspect the pending request.
9. Approve the quote as the human approver.
10. Return to the audit timeline to show inquiry analysis, product match, quote draft, approval request, and approval decision events.
